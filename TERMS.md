# Terms of Service: Iron Archive

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Iron Archive is a private tool of the YouTube channel **Classified 1945**. It sends the channel's own short videos
about World War II to the channel owner's TikTok account as drafts. The owner reviews every draft in the TikTok app
and decides whether to post it.

1. **Who can use it.** Only the channel owner's own TikTok account is connected. Nobody else can sign in or use it.
2. **What it does.** With the owner's permission (TikTok Login Kit, scope `video.upload`) it uploads a video file to
   the owner's TikTok inbox. It never posts anything publicly by itself and never reads other users' data.
3. **Content.** The videos are made by the channel from public-domain and freely licensed historical material
   (Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons, archives); sources are credited in the video descriptions.
4. **Ending access.** The owner can disconnect the tool at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security →
   Apps and services (Manage app permissions).
5. **No warranty.** The tool is provided as is, free of charge.
