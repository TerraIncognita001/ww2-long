# Privacy Policy: Iron Archive

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Iron Archive is a private tool of the YouTube channel **Classified 1945** that sends the channel's own videos to the
channel owner's TikTok account as drafts.

- **Data we receive from TikTok:** only what is needed to upload: an access token and refresh token, the account's
  open ID and the granted scopes (`user.info.basic`, `video.upload`). We do not collect any other personal data and
  no data of other TikTok users.
- **How it is used:** only to upload the channel's videos to the owner's own TikTok inbox.
- **Storage:** tokens are kept as encrypted secrets of the project (GitHub Actions secrets and an encrypted file).
  They are never shared, sold or used for advertising.
- **Deletion:** revoke access in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services); the stored tokens
  then stop working and are deleted on request.
- **Children:** the tool is not meant for and does not process data of children.
- **Contact:** https://github.com/TerraIncognita001/ww2-bot/issues
